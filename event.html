Join us for our bi-weekly meetings and our monthly Socials

Bi-Weekly Meetings: We'll be holding bi-weekly meetings to discuss club activities, plan events, and make decisions as a group. These meetings are a chance to share ideas, collaborate on projects, and ensure everyone is up-to-date.

Monthly Socials: In addition to our bi-weekly meetings, we'll also have monthly social events! These are more casual get-togethers to help us bond, network, and have fun outside of our formal meetings. Think game nights, movie nights, potlucks—we're open to suggestions!

Why this schedule? The bi-weekly meetings ensure we stay focused on our goals and make steady progress. The monthly socials help build camaraderie and a stronger sense of community within our club.
