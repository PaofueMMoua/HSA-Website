Hmong Student Association UC Merced

Who are we

Hmong Student Association (HSA) at UC Merced is a vibrant and welcoming community dedicated to supporting and empowering Hmong students on campus. We strive to create a supportive environment where students can connect with their cultural heritage, build lasting friendships, and achieve academic success. We achieve this through a variety of events, workshops, and activities that celebrate Hmong culture, provide academic support, and foster leadership development. We warmly encourage all students, regardless of background, to join us and experience the richness of our community. HSA is a place where everyone can find a sense of belonging, connect with their community, and thrive in their college journey.
