Hmong Student Association UC Merced

Who are we?

Hmong Student Association (HSA) at UC Merced is a vibrant and welcoming community dedicated to supporting and empowering students through the Hmong Culture. We strive to create a supportive environment where students can connect with their cultural heritage, build lasting friendships, and achieve academic success. We achieve this through a variety of events, workshops, and activities that celebrate Hmong culture, provide academic support, and foster leadership development. We warmly encourage all students, regardless of background, to join us and experience the richness of our community. HSA is a place where students can build lasting friendships, achieve academic support, and connect with the Hmong heritage.

What do we do?

Fostering unity

We celebrate cultural pride among all students.

Promoting Higher Education

We focus on encouraging everyone to strive for higher education.

Promote Awareness

Celebrating Hmong heritage on campus.

Our ground Rules

Ib Tsev Neeg - One Family

We are one big family, We stand together and we are eachother's home away from home.

Open Arms

We are open to everyone. The more the better.

One Mic

We respect whoever is talking and take turns speaking.

Be Humble and Proud

We know where we came from and we are Proud of how far we have come since then.

Uplift and Inspire

We help eachother and we inspire eachother to reach greater heights and better eachother.
