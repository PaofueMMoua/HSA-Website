Current Officers

See our Lovely Officers here at HSA from UC Merced

President - Louis Vang


Leading our organization with unwavering dedication is Louis Vang. With a passion for bringing attention to Hmong people at UC Merced, He brings a wealth of experience in office through being HSA's previous Historian. Louis's vision is to connect everyone together, fostering a collaborative environment that empowers our members, and drives impactful change. He is committed to making everyone feel like one big family no matter what. Louis ensures our organization remains a force for good in the community.

Vice President - Nicholas Vang

Our Vice President supports our President and assumes presidential duties in their absence. They often oversee specific committees or projects, assist with organizational planning, and may act as a liaison between the organization and other groups including disagreements within the organization itself.


Secretary - Natalie Vang


The Secretary is responsible for maintaining accurate records of meetings, managing correspondence, and handling administrative tasks. This includes taking minutes, managing membership lists, and distributing information to members. Essential qualities are strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and excellent communication abilities.

Treasurer - Grace Vang

The Treasurer manages the organization's finances, including budgeting, tracking income and expenses, overseeing preparation, execution of fundraisers, and preparing financial reports. They ensure the financial health of the organization and compliance with financial regulations. Key qualities include financial literacy, accuracy, and responsibility.


Public Relations - Kenneth Thao



The Public Relations officer is responsible for promoting the organization and its activities to the public and manages and maintains HSA's network of connections both on and off campus. This includes managing the organization's online presence, creating promotional materials, and communicating with the media. Essential qualities are strong communication skills, creativity, and the ability to build relationships.

Activities Director - Aubrey Yang


The Activities Director plans and coordinates events and activities for the organization's members. This includes organizing social events, workshops, and other activities that promote member engagement. Along with this the activities director oversees HSA's mentorship program called 'Big and Little' Key qualities include creativity, organizational skills, and the ability to work well with others.


Historian - Paofue Moua




The Historian is responsible for documenting the organization's history including its activities, achievements, and significant events. This includes maintaining records, collecting photos and videos, and creating historical archives. Essential qualities are attention to detail, organizational skills, and a passion for preserving history.

Director of Community Events - Kate Saechao


The Director of Community Events has 2 people and these people mainly plans and executes events that benefit the community. This includes organizing volunteer opportunities, fundraising events, and other initiatives that support the organization's mission. This includes our major events like Hmong Culture Night and our Educational Conference. Key qualities include organizational skills, community engagement, and the ability to work collaboratively.
